Delete your Bleu account

You can delete your Bleu account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Bleu and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm (you may be asked to sign in again).

By email

If you can't open the app, email hibleubleu@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Bleu account", from the email address you use for Bleu (or tell us how you signed in). We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account, profile (name, age, gender, who you'd like to meet, 2 AM line, interests) and online status
  • Your saved drawings and memes
  • Your notification token

What may be kept

  • Messages you already sent stay in the other person's chat history, as a text you sent stays on someone's phone. They are no longer shown with your profile.
  • Reports and blocks may be kept for up to 12 months to keep the community safe and to handle complaints.
  • Subscription records are kept by Apple, Google and our payment provider RevenueCat as required for tax and accounting. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

  • Saved drawings and memes: in the Memes tab, hold a drawing or meme, then tap ⊖. It's deleted straight away.
  • Profile details (name, 2 AM line, interests, who you'd like to meet): edit or clear them any time in the Me tab. The old version is replaced straight away.
  • Anything else (for example a specific chat): email hibleubleu@gmail.com from the email you use for Bleu, saying what you'd like deleted. We'll do it within 30 days. Purchase records and safety reports are kept as described above.
