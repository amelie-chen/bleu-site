Bleu

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Bleu is a photo-free late-night dating and chat app for adults aged 18 and over. This policy explains what information Bleu collects, why, who helps us process it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Bleu apps for iPhone and Android and to this website.

Who we are

Bleu is run by Xiaotao (Amelie) Chen, an independent developer based in France, who is the data controller for your information. Contact: hibleubleu@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a sign-in ID from Apple, Google or email sign-in. If you use "Hide My Email" with Apple, we only receive Apple's relay address.
  • Your profile: display name, age, gender (man or woman), who you'd like to meet, your "2 AM line" and up to three interests. Other Bleu users can see your profile, but not your email address.
  • Activity status: whether you're online and when you were last active, shown to other users.
  • Messages and creations: chats, stickers, drawings and memes you send, and drawings and memes you save in the app.
  • Safety information: people you block, and reports you make or that are made about you.
  • Notifications: a device notification token, if you turn notifications on.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Salon Privé, and purchase records from Apple or Google. We never see or store your card details.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: if the app crashes or hits an error, technical details such as device model, operating system version, app version and what the app was doing, so we can fix bugs.

Bleu does not collect photos of you, your contacts, or your precise location, and has no advertising.

Your choice of who you'd like to meet may reveal your sexual orientation, which the law treats as sensitive. We only use it to show you relevant people and to show your profile to the right people, and we rely on your explicit consent, which you give by choosing it. You can change it in your profile or delete your account at any time.

How we use it, and our legal bases

  • To run Bleu (your account, profile, Discover, chats, notifications and Salon Privé): to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep Bleu safe (18+ only, reports, blocks, stopping abuse): our legitimate interests, and legal obligations where they apply.
  • To fix crashes and improve the app: our legitimate interest in a working, reliable app.
  • Notifications: your consent, which you can withdraw in the app (Me → Notifications) or in your phone settings.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us (service providers)

We use these companies to run Bleu. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland / Google LLC): sign-in, database (stored in London, UK), and notifications.
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, app stores, payments and push notifications.
  • RevenueCat (USA): manages Salon Privé subscriptions using an anonymous ID linked to your account.
  • Expo (650 Industries, USA): delivers app updates.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, USA): crash and error reports.

Some of these providers are in the United States. Where data leaves the UK or the EU, transfers are protected by the UK and EU approved safeguards, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK extension) or standard contractual clauses.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of people using Bleu.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, and saved drawings and memes: until you delete your account.
  • Messages you sent: they remain in the other person's chat history after you delete your account, as a sent text stays on someone's phone.
  • Reports and blocks: up to 12 months after your account is deleted, for safety.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as Apple, Google and RevenueCat must keep them for tax and accounting.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your information, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. You can edit your profile and delete your account directly in the app. For anything else, email hibleubleu@gmail.com; we'll reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Adults only

Bleu is only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 is using Bleu, we'll delete their account.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, and access is limited by security rules so that, for example, only the people in a chat can read it. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share anything in Bleu that you'd regret being seen.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
