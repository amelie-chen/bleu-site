Common questions

How do I report or block someone?
Tap ••• at the top right of their profile or chat, then choose Report or Block. Blocked people can't see you or message you.
How do I cancel Salon Privé?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bleu. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bleu. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the paid month.
I changed phones. How do I get Salon Privé back?
Sign in with the same account, then go to Me → Restore purchases.
